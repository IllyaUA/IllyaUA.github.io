About

Scientist turned architect

I started as the scientist frustrated by LIMS integrations that never worked and instruments that couldn't talk to each other. Now I build the systems that fix it.

For more than ten years I have designed workflow orchestration and data pipelines across pharma, biotech and industrial R&D, from OPC UA device drivers to REST APIs to ML-based QC. I speak both languages: scientist and architect.
